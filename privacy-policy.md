# Privacy Policy - Currency Converter

Last updated: 5 September 2026

## The short version

I don't collect anything. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. I have no idea who uses this and I want to keep it that way.

## What gets saved

Your settings, in your own browser:

- Which currencies you picked
- Which ones you saved
- Language, theme, size, transparency, font
- Whether the extension is on or off
- The last exchange rates it fetched, so it doesn't have to ask again every time

That's it, and it sits on your machine. If you're signed in to Chrome with sync on, Chrome carries these settings between your own devices, the same way it does with bookmarks. I never see any of it.

## What actually leaves your computer

Two things, and only when they're needed.

**The exchange rate.** When you convert a price, the extension asks a public rates service what the rate is. The request is the amount and the two currency codes. Nothing else. Not the page you're on, not who you are.

- ExchangeRate-API, https://www.exchangerate-api.com
- currency-api as a backup, https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api

**Flag images.** The currency list shows flags, loaded from flagcdn.com. Same as any image on any website.

Both services see the request itself, which includes your IP, because that's how the internet works. What they do with that is up to them and covered by their own policies. Neither one gets anything about you from me, because I don't send anything about you.

## What the extension can see

It runs on web pages so it can notice when you highlight a price. It reads the text you selected. That's the whole extent of it. It doesn't read the rest of the page, doesn't keep a list of where you've been, and doesn't send page content anywhere.

## Why it needs the permissions it asks for

**Access to all websites.** Prices show up everywhere, so it has to be able to run everywhere. It does nothing at all until you highlight something.

**Storage.** To remember your settings so you don't have to set them again every time.

## Kids

This isn't aimed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone anyway.

## If this changes

If I ever change this policy, the new version goes up at this same address and the date at the top changes.
